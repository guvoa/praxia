What this is

Sixteen dot fields built on the spacing curve from the density write-up. Spacing grows on a curve while dot size stays flat, which is what makes a field read as settling rather than fading.

What is in the download

  • 16 fields as layered SVG, each with a light and a dark variant.
  • A Figma file with the fields as components and the curve as variables.
  • PNG exports at 1x, 2x and 3x for the four most-used sizes.
  • A one-page note on where the effect breaks — below 320px, and under motion.

Licence in plain words

Personal covers your own work. Team covers everyone at one company. Extended covers products you sell, including templates and apps. Nothing expires, and updates are free for a year.
