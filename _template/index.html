Post by Ana Costa

Ana Costa @anacosta
1d· Public

We cut the onboarding survey from twelve questions to four and the completion rate went from 31% to 78%. That is the headline, but the interesting part is which eight we cut and why nobody wanted to be the one to cut them. Every question had a person behind it who could explain exactly why it mattered, and every one of those explanations was reasonable on its own. The problem was never any single question. It was that twelve reasonable questions add up to something nobody will finish. So we stopped arguing about which questions were good and started asking which four we would keep if we could only keep four. That turned a two month argument into a forty minute meeting, and the four we kept are the ones we now use to route people into the right first week. The eight we dropped are still written down. We just ask them later, when someone has a reason to answer.

1.9K 128 comments · 44 reposts
