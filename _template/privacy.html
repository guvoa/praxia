Privacy notice

The short version: we collect what the product needs to work, we do not sell it, and you can take it with you.

Last updated 4 August 2026

What we collect

  • Account details: name, handle, email, and anything you add to your profile.
  • Content: posts, comments, reactions, messages, and the media you upload.
  • Technical data: device type, browser, and rough location from your IP address.

Why we collect it

To show your posts to the audience you chose, to keep accounts secure, and to review reports. We do not build advertising profiles because we do not sell advertising.

Who sees it

Other people see what your audience setting allows. Our staff see content only when a report is filed or the law requires it, and that access is logged.

Your controls

  • Change any post's audience after publishing.
  • Download everything you have posted, as a single archive, from settings.
  • Deactivate for 30 days, or delete permanently at any point.

Retention

Deleted posts leave our systems within 30 days, including backups. Report records are kept for a year so repeat behaviour can be spotted.
