Terms of use

Use Sovix to share your work and respond to other people's. Do not use it to harm anyone.

Last updated 4 August 2026

Your account

You need to be 16 or older. Keep your password to yourself, and tell us if you think someone else has it.

Your content

It stays yours. You give us permission to store it and show it to the audience you selected, and nothing beyond that. Delete it and the permission ends.

What is not allowed

  • Harassment, threats, or content that attacks people for who they are.
  • Posting someone else's work as your own.
  • Scraping the network, or automating accounts without written permission.

When we act

Reports are reviewed by people. We may remove content, limit reach, or close an account. You can appeal once, and a different reviewer reads it.

Changes

If these terms change in a way that affects you, we tell you at least 30 days before it takes effect, in the app and by email.
